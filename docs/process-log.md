A running record for the process & methodology document: what was built when, and every place where something (AI output, tooling or the source material) was wrong, how it was caught and how it was fixed.

## Verification approach

- **ROI:** 68 Vitest tests, exact equality, 1,208 sheet-evaluated cases. Mutation-checked: a one-digit rate change fails them.
- **UI behaviour:** Playwright scripts driving headless Chrome against the production build. Header: 34 checks covering hover intent delay, click toggle, outside click, Escape and focus return, scroll close, Tab/Enter/Space, sticky offset, drawer focus trap, scroll lock, touch target size, and no horizontal overflow at 10 widths from 360 to 1920. Lead left panel and marquee: 28 checks covering identical copy widths, copy wider than the viewport, fixed height, lazy images with dimensions, hover pause/resume, reduced motion, and CLS = 0 at 1440/768/375.
- **Lead form:** 53 schema unit tests (every message, 21 free providers incl. country variants, look-alike business domains that must pass, per-country phone rules). 33 browser checks: Figma placeholders, label association, aria-invalid/describedby, blur and submit validation, keyboard dropdowns and country typeahead, aria-disabled until valid, loading → failure (kept input) → retry → success with focus, honeypot and render time in the payload, server-side 400 with field messages, 405 for GET, honeypot bland success, no overflow at 375.
- **ROI UI:** 14 browser checks. Displayed numbers are parsed back and compared with the **sheet-evaluated fixtures** (not our code): first-load default, 16 sampled grid combinations, Tier 3 + Multi, min (negative savings) and max inputs, live update, keyboard toggle/slider, details panel, no overflow at 375. 23 format unit tests, including a check that every money value in the 1,208 fixtures displays the same rupees and paise as the sheet.
- **Footer:** 15 browser checks: link groups, social aria-labels, legal text, `<address>`, `tel:` links, placeholder links don't jump, hover animations, newsletter empty/invalid/valid states, no overflow at 375/768.
- **Page audit:** overflow at 10 widths (360–1920), one h1, all 47 images with alt and dimensions, lazy below the fold, LCP measured, 27 Tab stops with visible focus, axe-core 0 violations.
- **Fidelity:** screenshots compared side by side with the Figma section renders in `docs/design/screens/`.
